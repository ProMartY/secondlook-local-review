03 / HUMAN REVIEW

Look at both sides.

THE METHOD

Evidence first.
Decisions stay yours.

01

Start with the details

Compare the same vendor and currency. Related descriptions are only considered when amounts are within one cent and dates are at most 14 days apart.

02

Read beyond exact words

A small open model compares the meaning of descriptions. Repeated invoice IDs are flagged separately, including older records.

03

Keep the context

Two legitimate invoices can look alike. Check scope, service periods and payment records before you act. The app never changes a payment.
